# Recursos del atlas de campo

## forest-banner.webp

Ilustración ornamental generada con la herramienta integrada imagegen. Optimizada a 1800 × 600, WebP. No es una fotografía ni una identificación taxonómica de referencia.

Prompt: «Panoramic antique botanical field atlas engraving of Argentine southern forest. Deep forest green #172C23, layered araucaria and southern beech silhouettes, sage #87917A etched linework, warm ivory highlights. Forest and mountain ridges in the lower third; dark negative space above for HTML headlines. Fern and cannabis herbarium specimens alongside native plants, understated scientific context. Fine woodcut texture. No lettering, UI, borders or watermark. Landscape 3:1.»
