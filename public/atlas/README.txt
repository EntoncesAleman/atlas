Atlas visual asset conventions

Structure:
public/atlas/
  categories/
  fundamentals/
  cycle/
  environment/
  health/
  space/
  geography/

Naming:
category-{slug}.svg

Rules:
- Use cartographic texture and paper editorial texture.
- Avoid generic cannabis stock images.
- Represent workflow with abstract atlas panels, not fake photographic assets.
- Keep alt and accessible captions in UI text.
